# 10 — UI/UX, Progression, Economy and Accessibility

## 10.1 UI philosophy

**The HUD is a chalk mark, not a dashboard.** Everything is drawn in code as a
mark someone could have made with nzu on a wall. No rounded rectangles, no drop
shadows, no gradients, no icons that look like they came from a UI kit.

**Rules:**
1. **Nothing on the HUD moves unless it is telling you something.** The ọfọ bar
   shimmers only at full. Health flashes only below 25%.
2. **Text is never a wall.** A banner is one sentence. A cutscene beat is three at
   most. A codex entry is 80 words.
3. **Two fonts, never mixed in a line.** Serif is the world speaking. Sans is the
   game speaking.
4. **The game never tells you where to go.** No markers, no compass, no quest log.
5. **Every prompt names its key.** "↓ rest and save", "E Ala's ledger", "Z". On
   touch, the buttons carry the same letters so a prompt saying "Z" is findable.

## 10.2 The screens

| Mode | Entry | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `title` | boot | Continue (with progress), Begin Again, Speedrun, Codex, How to Fight |
| `play` | — | The game |
| `cut` | scripted | Letterboxed, typewriter text, painted art, voice blips, X to skip |
| `map` | M / icon | Baked room thumbnails, visited only, exits gold, mirrors blue |
| `pause` | ESC / ☰ | Resume · In hand · Ọfọ · What you carry · Codex · Map · Sound · Cheats · Save · Title |
| `inv` | pause | Weapons, spells with levels, everything taken, world completion |
| `codex` | title or pause | Two tabs: lore (story-gated) and bestiary (kill-gated) |
| `shop` | E at a charm | Ala's Ledger |
| `travel` | ↓ at an attuned mirror | Fast travel list |
| `riddle` | ↓ at an unattuned mirror | Gwam gwam gwam |
| `ending` | Onwe's death | The ending and the stats card |

**Mode rules:** every mode needs a `case` in the frame switch, a branch in the
render dispatch, and an entry in `MENU_MODES` — the last one is what makes the
joystick snap to a single direction and hold-to-scroll work. Forgetting it is the
most common bug when adding a screen.

**The joystick.** Two behaviours: in `play` it is analog with a 9px deadzone,
horizontal priority, and down requiring a deliberate 1.6× push. In any menu mode
it **snaps to one direction** with a 7px deadzone and does not re-fire until the
direction changes. It occupies only the lower-left corner so it can never
intercept the HUD icons.

## 10.4 Progression

**The critical path:** charm → path → shaft (learn the name) → Ogbunabali (Ala's
Fall) → bone road → Ekwensu (opens the gate) → fire (firebrand) → sky → Ala Mmụọ
→ Onwe → ending.

**Optional:** market, water (heart shard, mirror), all four mirrors, all ledger
purchases, the codex.

**Gates and their keys:**

| Gate | Key | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The chasm in Ọhịa | Air-dash | Aka Amadioha |
| Ogbunabali's mortality | His name | Nzu in the shaft |
| Cracked floors | Ala's Fall | Ogbunabali's death |
| The bone road's far exit | Ekwensu dead | `needs:'ekwensu'` |
| Fast travel | Riddles answered | The mirrors |

**Gate rule:** every gate's key is found *before* the gate on the critical path,
and the gate is *visible* before the key is found. The player must experience
wanting through something before getting the thing that opens it.

**Power curve:** the player's damage roughly triples across a run (9 → ~34 with a
charged heavy at Ikenga III). Enemy health roughly quadruples (28 → 520+). The
gap is closed by *skill*, not stats — which is why heart shards cap at 3 and there
are no levels.

## 10.5 Economy

**Cowries** drop from everything, arc out physically, and magnetise to the player
within 44px. Death drops them as a **shade** you can walk back to and recover.

| Source | Yield |
|---|---|
| Thrower | 12 |
| Walker / crawler / ember | 15 |
| Warden / effigy | 26 |
| Boss | 260 |
| Cowrie Charm skill | ×1.5 |

**Sinks:** spells (70/170/340 per level), weapons (190, 260), gourds (110 each),
heart shards (130/260/390), skills (160–230).

**Total available in a full clear:** roughly 3,000–4,000. Total cost of
everything: roughly 4,600. **This is deliberate** — a single playthrough cannot
buy everything, so the player has to commit to a build, and NG+ has a reason to
exist.

## 10.6 Accessibility

Not a difficulty menu (§01.8). Options that remove *barriers* without removing
the game.

**Implemented:**
- Speedrun mode — an honest, labelled escape valve for anyone who wants the story
  without the fight
- Input buffering through hitstop, so a press is never eaten by a freeze frame
- Coyote time (7f) and jump buffer (8f)
- Every prompt names its key; touch buttons carry the same letters
- Colour is never the *only* channel: gold tells also have a **doubled** outline
  and a faster pulse, so the white/gold read survives colour blindness

**Committed, not yet built** (Phase 2, §12):
- **Screen shake slider** (0–100%). Highest-value accessibility feature we do not
  have; vestibular sensitivity is common and shake is heavy here.
- **Grain and flash toggles**
- **Hold-to-ward alternative** so the parry is not a 9-frame reflex test — a
  toggle that widens the window to 14 frames, labelled honestly as *Slow Ward*
- **Text size scale** (1×, 1.25×, 1.5×)
- **High-contrast tells** — thicker outlines, higher-contrast colours
- **Remappable keys**
- **Auto-cast on ọfọ full** for one-handed play

**Rule:** every accessibility option is *named for what it does*, never for the
kind of player it is for. "Slow Ward," not "Assist Mode."

## 10.7 Quest design

Ọdịnala has no quest system and will not get one. What it has instead:

- **Standing questions.** Who paid Ekwensu. What is behind the fourth mirror.
  These live in the player's head, not in a log.
- **Object-driven objectives.** A locked gate. A cracked floor. A boss you cannot
  hurt. The objective *is* the obstacle.
- **The codex as the only tracker.** It shows what you have found, never what you
  have not.

If a future feature genuinely needs a multi-step objective, it must be expressible
as a **physical trail** — a series of chalk marks, a debt recorded in cowries at
the market — never as a UI list.
